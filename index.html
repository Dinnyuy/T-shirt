A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good Questions.

Everything starts with a conversation.

How do I order a T-shirt, sweatshirt or hoodie? ＋

Choose a design and submit the interest form. Our team contacts you to confirm the details before an order is agreed.

Can I customize my own design? ＋

Yes. Select Custom Creations or customize a sample, then share your exact wording, colours, style and print placement. Designs are subject to approval.

Can I provide my own Bible verse or phrase? ＋

Yes. Include the exact text, Scripture reference and preferred translation. Please check spelling, capitalization and punctuation carefully.

Can I order for my church or organization? ＋

Yes. Choose Group / Church Orders and share your group name, quantity, size breakdown and event details. The team will discuss your requirements.

What sizes and colours are available? ＋

The form lets you indicate your preferred size and colour. These are requests, not confirmed stock; availability and fit are confirmed before production.

How much do the T-shirts, sweatshirts and hoodies cost? ＋

Pricing is confirmed individually based on the garment, design and quantity. No payment is collected on this website.

How long does production take? ＋

The team confirms the production timeline with you after reviewing your request. If you have an event date, include it in your notes.

Do you offer delivery? ＋

Indicate delivery, collection or to be discussed. The team confirms available fulfilment arrangements and any applicable costs directly.
